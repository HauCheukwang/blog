## Shanyu Liang / 梁善予

From the read-to-write experience, I have learned that accumulation is the basis of our critical writing. When reading a research paper, we can not just understand the content of the text, but observe how the author presents arguments and accumulate some common structures useful for our own writing.

What's more, every word of the research paper should be carefully considered, because each word implies the author's attitude and affects the scientific nature and rigour. Besides, we should pay attention to the use of linking words, which make the logic of our paper clearer.

Finally, forming the habit of critical reading and thinking is beneficial to our lives. We can associate the phenomenon of the paper with profound causes. This ability adds greater depth and practical significance to our writing.

## XIanhui Wang / 汪贤辉

In this reading-to-write project, our group chose the song Yesterday Once More. As we went through its lyrics, I learned some natural, everyday expressions, including long-lost friend, look back on and years gone by.

Apart from native expressions, the lyrics also gave me fresh ideas about writing. I've found that good writing does not always depend on fancy words or complex sentences. We can share our feelings with concrete images like radios, old songs and familiar melodies. It works well if we notice small details and express our thoughts in simple, smooth language.

On the other hand, this activity changed how I listen to music. Before, I usually just enjoyed the melody and seldom thought deeply about what a song tries to tell us. But in this project, I figured out its core idea: nostalgia does not mean staying stuck in the past, memories bring us soft comfort.

Last but not least, this group work helped me realize that English reading and writing, are far more than memorizing vocabulary or translating sentences. We need to feel the emotions behind the words, and think carefully about how our memories connect with the present.

## Zhuohong Hou / 侯卓宏

Needless to say, learning the course of Critical Reading has taught me a lot of useful vocabulary such as exploitative, stance, utilize and so on and some useful expression like "it is my contention that" which is a useful expression to argue that perspective that you want to argue.

What most important is that I realize that reading or writing is not picking every words into a context but connecting each words with "R-D-I"(Restatement-Description-Interpretation) to express your critical thinking. So I will regard "R-D-I" logic flow as a useful tool to get author's exact conveys and express my critical thinking and apply it on my next writing and reading.

Not only is the course of critical reading an English class that taught me many English skills, it also conveys me to an thinking mode which is critical thinking, an ability to explore "from say to mean"(say-do-mean).

## Zhaoqi Wang / 王昭麒

From this activity ,I learn that we don't just need to learn English as a new language.What's more,you should understand it as a culture. Maybe it is different from our culture, but we should learn to understand, respect and accept it. Each of his words has different meanings.

Before I came into contact with critical reading, my study of English was limited to memorizing words and reading. Just to complete the questions, and after I learned critical reading, I began to face my views on English, and no longer studied for the sake of doing questions to get points.

Many articles seem to be just simple narratives and discussions, but in fact they imply personal attitudes and cultural tendencies. Reading with critical thinking can help me jump out of the surface of the text, see the deeper expression logic, and avoid one-sided and shallow understanding.

## 编者点评（组长归纳）

五份反思虽短，却共同勾勒出这门课的核心收益——**英语写作课最终教的不是语言技巧，而是批判性思维**。我把它收敛成四条共识：

1. **从"工具性学习"到"文化性理解"**：汪贤辉、王昭麒都经历了从"背单词/刷题"到"把英语当文化来理解、尊重与接纳"的转变。英语不再是得分工具，而是理解另一种思维方式的窗口。
2. **R-D-I 框架的内化**：侯卓宏明确把 *Restatement–Description–Interpretation* 当作可迁移的"思维工具"，并进一步提炼出 *say–do–mean*（从"说—做—意"）的批判性路径——这正是课程最想植入的认知习惯。
3. **具体意象胜过华丽辞藻**：梁善予、汪贤辉都提到，用 radio、old songs、familiar melodies 这类**具体意象**去承载情感，比堆砌 fancy words 更有效。这对写作很有启发。
4. **批判性阅读溢出课堂**：梁善予把论文现象关联到深层成因，王昭麒学会跳出文本表面看表达逻辑——批判性思维开始反哺对生活与信息的判断。
