> **编者按（Cheukwang Hau）**：本篇是课程作业 **Part A** 的原文存档，作者为本人（Cheukwang Hau）。采用 **R-D-I 三步法**（Restatement 复述 → Description 描述 → Interpretation 阐释）对《Yesterday Once More》歌词做批判性阅读。以下正文为本人原创提交内容，**一字未改**地保留；末尾的"编者点评"才是我（作为博主/编者）事后回看时的再思考，两者界限明确，请勿混淆。

---

## Restatement

The song is about a speaker's memories of old songs. When young, the speaker used to listen to the radio, waiting for favourite songs and singing along. Now those old songs are back again, just like a long-lost friend, and every "sha-la-la" still shines. When the old songs reach the part where he is breaking her heart, the speaker can really cry, just like before. Looking back on the good times of years gone by makes today seem rather sad, because so much has changed, but the speaker still remembers every word of those old melodies. In the end, it is yesterday once more.

## Description

The song focuses on personal first-person memory instead of public events. It makes the feeling of nostalgia stay in one person's inner world. In the chorus, there are no clear words but meaningless sounds like "sha-la-la-la". These sounds do not carry special meanings. They just sound like old records. In the middle part of the song, we can hear a mix of popular songs from the 1960s. At this point, the song is no longer talking about old music. It shows the old music directly to listeners. Besides, the warm and soft alto voice and the retro music style also help. All these things make the song sound older than it really is, though it came out in 1973.

## Interpretation

After listening to such a warm yet slightly sad song, it can be argued that the songwriters aim not only to describe everyday scenes — the radio, the old songs and the "sha-la-la-la" refrains — but also to write down memories of the past. These memories are more than a record of "the good times" of "years gone by": they give the speaker the strength to face the present, because "all my best memories" still "come back clearly" whenever the old songs are played. However, the song is not simply comforting. Looking back also "makes today seem rather sad," since "so much has changed." In other words, the song suggests that remembering is bittersweet: the old songs return "just like a long lost friend," yet they can only return as "yesterday once more" — never as today.

## 编者点评（事后回看）

重读自己这篇 R-D-I，整体结构是规范的，几处观察也站得住脚，挑几点说：

**做得扎实的地方**
- **三步法执行到位**：Restatement 忠实不添加、Description 聚焦文本特征、Interpretation 落到个人阐释，三者分工清楚，是合格的批判性阅读范式。
- **Description 有两个真亮点**：一是点出 "sha-la-la-la" 无意义却能唤起老唱片质感——抓住了"形式即内容"；二是发现 medley 让歌曲从"谈论旧音乐"转为"直接呈现旧音乐"，这个媒介自觉的观察很细腻。
- **Interpretation 收尾有力**：把怀旧读成 *bittersweet*，既看到"面对当下的力量"，也承认"物是人非的伤感"，并以 "yesterday once more" 而非 "today" 收束，点出了怀旧的本质局限。

**可再深化的方向（建设性）**
- Description 段其实可以再区分一下叙事里的性别化线索——"he's breaking her heart" 暗示了一种传统的男性叙事视角，值得点一句。
- Interpretation 若想更厚，可以联系 1973 年的时代背景（越战尾声、经济滞胀）来读"怀旧"是否也是对动荡当下的一种逃避，而不只是个人感伤。

总体是一篇可以当范文的 R-D-I，结构比辞藻更重要。
